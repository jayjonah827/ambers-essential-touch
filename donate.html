The Mission

Gentle care for every season.

Donation-based facials for seniors and teens who may not have access to these resources.

SUPPORT THE MISSION

Why

Your skin has memory.

Every line, every touch, every season of life leaves an imprint. Skin carries records of laughter, stress, sun and resilience. On young skin, it is learning itself for the first time — changing, adjusting, healing.

Amber's Essential Touch is collecting donations to provide gentle, intentional skincare and self-care for seniors and teens who may not have access to these resources.

What Donations Fund

Direct, dignified care.

Facial sessions for teens learning how to care for new skin.

Wellness kits for seniors experiencing dryness and sensitivity.

Small-batch oils blended for sensitive and aging skin.

Community-based work in dignity and access.
