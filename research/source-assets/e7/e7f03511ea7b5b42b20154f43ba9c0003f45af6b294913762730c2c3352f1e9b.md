# Heyer Livin' — Design System

> ```
> Choice is not chance. The pattern is structural.
> ```

```
This is the design system for Heyer Livin', a cultural-intelligence and design studio founded by Jair Valley (also Jonah / Jay Jonah) in Oakland, California. It exists so any designer or agent can produce well-branded Heyer Livin' interfaces, decks, prints, and assets — either for production or for throwaway mocks.
```

It is *not* a moodboard. Every visual element has a job. The first rule of the system is binding:

> **Every asset must answer one of three functions: recognize, measure, or route.** If it does none of those, it is decoration. Decoration is allowed for editorial breathing room, but it is never the system layer.

## 1. What Heyer Livin' Is

Heyer Livin' is a studio that sits at the intersection of **design, anthropology/sociology, and research-tech**. It builds:

- **Brand & visual systems** — logos, wordmarks, glyph sheets, pattern systems, product/apparel mockups, editorial design. Built to survive on fabric, paper, screen, and packaging.
- **Cultural research** — structural inequality, symbolism, and social pattern translated into *measurable, reproducible artifacts* (the public proof set: 13 statistical tests, 12 independent datasets, one structural attractor at ≈ **0.39**).
- **Systems & tools** — operational design, documentation, AI-assisted research workflows, and a "Glyph Ecosystem" of autonomous nodes governed by the bounded-state ratio **R = x / (x + y²)**.

The founder's lived material runs through the work: Oakland, hospitality/operations (coffee, bar, hotel, night-audit), Business & Sociology at Berkeley City College, and a refusal to flatten Black cultural reality into "diversity." The mission frame: *make culture more legible without stripping it of complexity*, and surface the structural error in how data gets compressed — e.g. the documented blind spots in Black maternal health.

## 3. CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS — how Heyer Livin' writes

The voice is **precise, declarative, and unhurried**. It earns its specificity. It reads like a high-end broadsheet crossed with a scientific readout — never corporate-neutral, never wellness-soft.

**Tone & stance**

- **Recognition before prescription.** Copy names a structure the reader half-knew was there. It does not instruct or hype.
- **Declarative, short, weighted.** Sentences land like findings. *"Choice is not chance. The pattern is structural." / "One eye. Four directions." / "Heads: choice. Tails: erased."*
- **Evidence-forward.** Numbers are stated plainly and treated as proof: *"Twelve independent datasets. Thirteen statistical tests. One attractor at 0.39."*
- **Cultural specificity is non-negotiable.** Do **not** flatten Blackness into "diversity," soften *Choice Is Not Chance* into "choice and chance," or swap *erased* → "less visible," *measured* → "thoughtful," *the pattern is structural* → "patterns exist," *cultural intelligence* → "cultural awareness." The whole point is to resist compression.

**Person & address**

- Public marketing copy speaks **about the work in third person / studio voice** ("A cultural intelligence and design studio…", "What the studio does").
- The **About / founder voice is first person** ("I design cultural navigation systems.").
- Direct address to the reader ("you") is used sparingly and only for action/route copy ("Work with the studio," "Start here").

**Casing & mechanics**

- **Eyebrows / kickers:** UPPERCASE, wide-tracked — `EVIDENCE`, `WORK`, `RESEARCH`, `HEYER LIVIN' — OAKLAND`.
- **Headlines:** sentence case, tight tracking. Not title case, not all-caps.
- The apostrophe in **Heyer Livin'** is load-bearing — always include it (curly `'`).
- **Data/measurements** are written in the mono voice: `R = x / (x + y²)`, `≈ 0.39`, `13 TESTS`, `LOWER 0.30 / UPPER 0.50`.

**Vibe:** archival, exact, analog, dignified. Public surfaces may stay at "glyph-driven framework" or "symbolic navigation system" and hold back the internal math (Eye-Roglyph, AI(ci), zone math, S ⊗ I). **No emoji. No exclamatory hype. No SaaS gradient-speak.**

## 4. VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

### Two skins, one brand

**(A) Canonical / Structural — DEFAULT.** The brand palette is **black · white · red · blue** — and nothing else in this skin. Ground is **pure black** (`#000000`, no lightened variant). Marks and type are **white** (`#ffffff`). **Red** (`#E11D2E`) is the active-route / attention accent — used sparingly; if everything is red, nothing routes. **Blue** (`#1B45E5`) is the measurement / structural-reading accent, rarer still. (A darkened blue, cobalt `#0b1c3a`, serves as the blueprint field for technical surfaces.) *always* indicates something earning attention — the active route, the evidence accent. **Signal cyan** (`#00d1ff`) is rarer still: measurement, motion, structural overlays. **Cobalt** (`#0b1c3a`) is the blueprint field for research/process surfaces. This is the shipped homepage, the about page, the dashboard.

**(B) Archival Broadsheet — warm alternate.** Ground is **aged paper** (`#F6F2E9`, never pure white). **Archive Indigo** (`#1B2A4A`) is structural ink; **Glyph Ochre** (`#C97B3C`) is the human/analog signal; **Signal Teal** (`#2E6E66`) is "the structural reading / the gap." Cards are pure white on paper with hairline `slate-300` borders. Use for editorial / print-leaning / longform reading contexts.

### Color discipline

- **No gradients as ornament.** Gradients are reserved for data visualizations where they represent a measured continuum.
- **Accents are used sparingly** — red first (attention/route), blue rarer (measurement). If everything is accented, nothing routes.
- **State tier** (proof surfaces): `subordinated` blue `#1B45E5` (R<0.33), `structural` white (0.33–0.50), `dominant` red `#E11D2E` (R>0.50), `outer-field` gray `#555` (unscored).
- Imagery color vibe: either **high-contrast black & white** (logos, glyph sheets, line icons, fabric patterns) **or rich warm jewel-tones** (the stained-glass Tree-of-Knowledge hero — amber, forest green, cobalt, ruby). No washed-out, no cool desaturated stock. Grain/print texture welcome; glossy "AI render" sheen forbidden.

### Typography

- **Body / UI / sans:** **Helvetica Neue → Helvetica → Arial**. The most market-legible neutral sans; let the math and the glyphs differentiate.
- **Display / headlines / wordmark-in-type:** the **same Helvetica Neue**, set heavy (700) and tight (−0.03em). **No serif in live type.** The high-contrast editorial serif seen in the wordmark exists *only baked into the logo lockup image* (`assets/logo/`) — never reproduced as a webfont. Let the math and the glyphs differentiate, not a display face.
- **Data / measurement:** **IBM Plex Mono**, tabular numerics always on. This is why `0.390992` can sit next to `0.39` without optical jitter.
- Weights **400/500/600/700** only — no light, no black (except the serif wordmark, which goes heavy). Display is always bold.
- **Tracking is a system:** tight `-0.025em` on display/H1, snug `-0.015em` on H2/H3, `0` on body, wide `0.04em` on buttons, caps `0.12em` on nav, **label `0.2em`** on eyebrows.

### Spacing, grid, ratios

- **Spacing is multiplicative:** 0 · .25 · .5 · .75 · 1 · 1.5 · 2 · 3 · 5 · 8 rem. Steps grow into legible negative space at section scale.
- **Primary grid: 8 × 8** (64 cells) — logo sheets, glyph catalogs, poster/collection layouts.
- **Primary split: 39 / 61.** Wherever a page divides asymmetrically, the 0.39 attractor controls it (mark on the 39%, body on the 61%). It's a quiet signature on every page.
- **Radial work uses π/8 = 0.3927** — eight ticks, eight states (the origin glyph's eight outward marks).
- **Cycle cadence: 10 / 20 / 40** for motion and release rhythm.

### Borders, corners, shadow, depth

- **Border radius is `0` system-wide** in the canonical skin. The system is measured, not soft. (The archival-broadsheet skin allows a disciplined `4px`/`8px`.)
- Borders: thin `1px`, strong `2px`, **rule `3px`** (a 2–3px white rule introduces a section — it's a structural divider, not a soft separator).
- **No drop shadows on UI** in the canonical skin — depth is real only in product photography. The archival skin uses a **two-layer, background-derived** shadow (never pure black; children always shallower than parents).
- **Cards** in the canonical skin = a `1px` white (or hairline) border on ink, square corners, no shadow. In the archival skin = white surface on paper, soft layered shadow, hairline `slate-300` border, gentle radius.

### Backgrounds, transparency, blur

- Default ground is **flat ink, pure black, deep cobalt, or transparent** — not warm off-white in the canonical skin.
- **Fabric/line patterns** (dense mirrored B&W linework) are used as textile/packaging surfaces and occasional low-opacity backdrops — never loud behind reading copy.
- The user's recent landing-page experiment introduced **glassmorphism, grain, and serif headlines on dark** — treat that as an *editorial extension* of skin (A), not a replacement; keep blur/transparency tasteful and rare.

### Motion & states

- Durations **0 / 120 / 200 / 400ms**. Ease-out `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`; in-out `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`. No bounce, no spring, no parallax theatrics.
- **Hover** = a measured shift, not a glow: a red underline/border appears, or a primary fill flips paper → red (canonical) / ink → teal (archival). **Press** = subtle opacity/inset, not a squish.
- Focus rings are deliberate: 2px (red/blue in canonical, teal in archival).

## 5. ICONOGRAPHY

Iconography is the **core** of this brand — glyphs are treated as *navigational instruments*, not decoration. "A logo says *who*. A glyph says *how*."

### The eight system glyphs (`assets/glyphs/*.svg`)

Each is a **64 × 64 SVG, white stroke (`#ffffff`, 2px) on transparent ground**, square caps, miter joins, no rounded joins — calibrated to survive screen, print, fabric, and small product mockups at any scale ≥16px. They invert cleanly (toggle `stroke` to black on paper). **Never introduce midtones.**

| Glyph | Job | Use it for | Never |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Eye** | Recognition / witness | logo, headers, About, closing slide | corner decoration — the Eye is never small |
| **Clock** (circle) | Time as mechanism | roadmaps, cadence, Arcade, "now" status | a decorative clock with no real ticks |
| **Compass** (square) | Direction / frame | navigation, Filing, route guides | generic "guidance" copy |
| **Coin** (triangle) | Choice states | evidence prints, A/B, research summaries | pricing / money imagery |
| **Pyramid** | Hierarchy / field | constraint diagrams, power maps | aspirational climb-the-ladder imagery |
| **Origin** | Generator / seed | opening slides, center of a π/8 wheel | a generic bullet point |
| **Graph** | Distribution / cluster | research, proof blocks, the 0.39 convergence | generic "analytics" imagery |
| **Hand** | Human signal | contact, community, dignity surfaces | filler on empty surfaces |

**Combinations are working sentences:** Eye + Compass = "Look, then choose a direction." Clock + Coin = "This decision is on a cycle." Graph + Coin = "The choice produces a distribution." Hand + Eye = "A person is being seen here."

### Other icon assets (`assets/icons/`)

- **Four-line icon set** (`four-line-icons.png`) — Apple, Coin, Compass, Clock as no-fill, **same-stroke-weight** line drawings. The "Apple" (knowledge / Eden / Adam-and-Eve) joins the eight on storytelling surfaces.
- **Eye glyph with rays** (`eye-glyph-rays.png`) and **`eye.png`** — radiant eye marks; there are 15 hand-drawn eye-glyph variants in the source uploads.

### Approach & substitutions

- **Use the brand's own marks first** — the SVG glyphs and PNG icons here. They are bespoke; do **not** redraw them by hand or with emoji.
- **No emoji, ever.** No unicode pictographs as icons. Mathematical/structural unicode in the mono voice is fine where it's *content* (`≈`, `⊗`, `π/8`, `R = x/(x+y²)`).
- If a UI needs a utility icon not in the set (chevron, close, menu, arrow), use a **thin, square-cap, 2px stroke** line icon to match the four-line set — e.g. **Lucide** (`https://unpkg.com/lucide-static`) at `stroke-width:2`, square linecap. **Flag any such addition** — it is a substitution, not brand canon.

### Logos & marks (`assets/logo/`)

- **`heyer-livin-logo.png`** — primary lockup: "HEYER LIVIN'" in the high-contrast display serif, the **Eye** above, over a **wireframe-globe grid**, white-on-black. The anchor of the identity.
- **`monogram.png` / `final-monogram.png`** — interlocked **HL** with the eye + eight-point starburst.
- **`monogram-mono.png`**, **`wordmark-hrlvn.png`** — condensed marks for small/favicon use.
- Logos are **black-on-white or white-on-black only**. Keep clear space; never recolor, never add effects.

## 6. Font notes — ACTION OPTIONAL ⚠

**Body, UI, and display all use Helvetica Neue** (a system font) — no webfont needed. The **data/measurement voice is IBM Plex Mono** (named directly in `DESIGN.md`, loaded from Google Fonts). No serif font is loaded: the editorial serif appears **only inside the logo lockup image**, which ships as artwork.

If you ever want the serif wordmark reproduced as *editable type* (e.g. for a new lockup), the brand's display serif is served from **Adobe Typekit (kit `rod3osj`)** — **send the licensed font files / family name** and it can be wired in. Until then, the logo image is the source of truth for the serif.
