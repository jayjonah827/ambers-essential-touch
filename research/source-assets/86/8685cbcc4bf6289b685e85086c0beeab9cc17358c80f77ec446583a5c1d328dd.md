## Voice rules (enforced across every page)
1. Short declaratives. No long clauses.
2. Ampersand cadence — three things, comma-comma-period.
3. Italic serif on the emotional word.
4. Capitalized short phrases (PURELY ESSENTIAL, THE PRACTICE) as eyebrows.
5. Never "we" for solo-practitioner voice. Use "I" on About; "Amber's Essential Touch" on Home and Mission.
6. No "learn more." Use "read," "book," "join," "support," "explore."
7. No exclamation marks except in client quotes.
8. No emojis in body copy (✨ allowed only in featured FB-style quotes, and sparingly).
9. Never use "affordable," "luxurious," "pampering," "indulgence." Use "intentional," "gentle," "ritual," "aftercare," "patient."
10. Always close with a short phrase that returns to touch: *held, carry, care, begin.*
