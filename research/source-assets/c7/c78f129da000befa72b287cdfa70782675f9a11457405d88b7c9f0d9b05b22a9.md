# Portrait QA Checklist

Reject the output if any required item fails.

- [ ] The authoritative source and output are compared side by side at face scale.
- [ ] Face shape, features, spacing, expression, and apparent age match.
- [ ] Pores, fine lines, beauty marks, and skin tone remain natural.
- [ ] Loc pattern, texture, silhouette, and edge detail remain credible.
- [ ] Body, pose, hands, fingers, clothing, jewelry, and chair match the authorized source.
- [ ] No unintended retouching, reshaping, slimming, or makeup enhancement is visible.
- [ ] Background extraction has no halos, missing locs, hard cut edges, or spill.
- [ ] Direction, softness, and color of environmental light are physically plausible.
- [ ] Mirror reflections, plants, and water obey believable scale and depth.
- [ ] The image feels welcoming, experienced, grounded, and self-possessed.
- [ ] The output is versioned and recorded; originals remain unchanged.
- [ ] Amber or her designated approver has given final publication approval.
