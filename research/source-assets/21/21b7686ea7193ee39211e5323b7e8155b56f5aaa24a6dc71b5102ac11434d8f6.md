## Tone attributes
- **Grounded** — sentences land on the floor, not in the air.
- **Intentional** — nothing is accidental in the copy; no filler.
- **Culturally aware** — Black-woman owned, multi-generational skin, teen + senior mission.
- **Personal but not chaotic** — warm, not scattered.
- **Ritual over transaction** — "book a ritual," not "schedule a service."

## Rhythm patterns (use all four, rotate)
1. **Three-beat comma cadence:** *"Patience, repetition, and respect."*
2. **Short declarative stack:** *"Licensed Esthetician. Waxing specialist. Eighteen years. Still learning."*
3. **Italic emotional word:** *"Where Skin Care Becomes __Ritual__."*
4. **All-caps eyebrow + serif headline:** `PURELY ESSENTIAL` → *4 Ingredients. Infinite Possibilities.*

## Always use
- "ritual," "practice," "intentional," "gentle," "aftercare," "consultation," "regimen," "patience," "held," "carry," "skin," "memory," "protect"
- Italic serif for emotional accent word
- Ampersand or comma cadence (comma, comma, period)
- "I" on About page (solo practitioner voice)
- "Amber's Essential Touch" or "the practice" elsewhere

## Never use
- "luxurious," "indulgent," "pampering," "treat yourself," "spa day"
- "affordable," "cheap," "deal," "discount" (substitute "accessible," "by invitation," "seasonal")
- "learn more" (substitute "read," "explore," "book," "see")
- Exclamation marks in body copy (OK in client quotes)
- Emojis in headlines or product copy (✨ only sparingly in FB-style captions)
- "We" for solo-practitioner voice
- Em-dashes where a comma would do
- Medical or diagnostic claims ("cures," "treats," "heals acne" → substitute "supports," "calms," "softens," "clears congestion")

## Do / Don't examples

| DON'T | DO |
|---|---|
| "Luxurious facial experience!" | *Consultation-based facials. Aftercare included.* |
| "Buy our amazing oil today!" | *Purely Essential Virgin Oil. 4 ingredients. Shaving, cleansing, healing.* |
| "Learn more about our mission" | *Read the mission →* |
| "Treat yourself to a spa day" | *Book a ritual.* |
| "Our best-selling product" | *The one most people return for.* |
| "We care about your skin" | *Your skin has memory. I work with what it remembers.* |

## Voice checklist (use before publishing any page)
- [ ] Every headline has either an italic emotional word or an all-caps eyebrow
- [ ] No sentence is longer than 18 words unless it's on the About page
- [ ] At least one three-beat comma cadence per section
- [ ] CTA is a verb, not a sentence: `BOOK`, `READ`, `JOIN`, `SUPPORT`
- [ ] No "we," no "learn more," no exclamation marks in body
- [ ] Closing line of the page returns to touch, held, carry, or care
- [ ] No forbidden words: luxurious, indulgent, pampering, treat yourself

## Newsletter voice (Skin Notes — monthly)
- Subject line formula: `Skin Notes · {month} · {one-phrase ritual}`
  - Example: *Skin Notes · April · Patience, Pressed In*
- Greeting: `Dear skin,` or `Hello, gentle reader.`
- 3 sections max: What I'm making · What I'm seeing · What's open this week
- Closing: signature "— Amber" and one soft sentence (*"Take your time. Your skin will."*)

## Social voice (Instagram + Facebook)
- Caption cadence: one short line, one gap, one short line, one gap, one CTA
- Hashtag count: 5 max, always end with `#ambersessentialtouch #pinole`
- Never repost generic stock quotes
- Every 4th post is a client (anonymized or consented) story
- Every 7th post is a donation-mission post
