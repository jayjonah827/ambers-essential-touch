## 4. Phase-2 triggers (do NOT build until triggered)

- Stripe checkout on Wix: trigger = 3 inbound product inquiries in one week
- Paid Wix plan + custom domain: trigger = first $100 in bookings through site
- SMS (Twilio/Mailchimp SMS): trigger = email list > 250
- Group sessions / workshops: trigger = 10 repeat 1:1 clients in 30 days
- Affiliate / wholesale: trigger = 3 unsolicited wholesale requests

## 5. Three revenue streams (map to pages)

1. **Product** → Shop page, Product detail pages, Bundles card on Home
2. **Service** → Services page, Appointments page, Monthly Rituals card on Home
3. **Support / community** → Donate page (teen + senior facials), Email list capture everywhere

## 6. What the weekly agent does (runs every Monday 8:00a PT)

1. Reads Notion `Content Log` + `Service Log` databases for entries tagged this week
2. Pulls latest 1 blog post + 1 featured product + booking availability for next 14 days + donation progress
3. Drafts a newsletter in MailerLite with the subject line formula: *"Skin Notes — {week of X} — {one-sentence ritual}"*
4. Saves a draft (never auto-sends) — Amber approves from MailerLite mobile app
5. Logs the draft back into Notion `Newsletter Log` database

## 7. What is out of scope (Phase 1)

- Native e-commerce checkout
- Paid ads
- SMS
- Affiliate program
- Multi-location anything
- Custom CMS / Webflow / Shopify migration
- Any non-Wix storefront
