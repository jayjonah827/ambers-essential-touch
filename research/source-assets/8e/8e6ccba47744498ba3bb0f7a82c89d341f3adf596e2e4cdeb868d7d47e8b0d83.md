### Typography
| Token | Family | Size / weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `display-script` | **Dancing Script** or **Pinyon Script** | 48–96px, regular | "Amber's" wordmark, italic display accents |
| `display-serif` | **Cormorant Garamond** | 40–72px, 300/400 | H1, section H2 |
| `display-serif-italic` | **Cormorant Garamond Italic** | same | Emotional accent word within H1/H2 |
| `body-serif` | **Cormorant Garamond** or **Playfair Display** | 16–20px, 400 | Body paragraphs |
| `ui-sans` | **Inter** or **Montserrat** | 11–14px, 500, letter-spacing 0.12em | Eyebrows, labels, CTAs, nav |
| `ui-sans-lg` | same | 14–16px, 500 | Buttons |

All caps: eyebrows, CTAs, nav, stats labels.

### Spacing scale (8pt grid)
`4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128`

### Radii
- `radius-none`: 0 (buttons, cards by default)
- `radius-sm`: 2px (form fields)
- `radius-md`: 8px (images within cards)
- `radius-pill`: 999px (tags, category chips)

### Shadows (used sparingly)
- `shadow-none` (default)
- `shadow-soft`: `0 8px 24px rgba(59, 36, 23, 0.08)` — product cards only

### Borders
- `border-hairline`: `1px solid amber-300/40`
- `border-accent`: `1px solid amber-500`

## 2. Component library (Figma components, with variants)

### Button
Variants: `primary` · `secondary` · `ghost` · `text-link`
States: default · hover · pressed · focus · disabled
Sizes: `sm` (36h) · `md` (44h) · `lg` (52h)

- **Primary** — filled `amber-500`, text `cream-50`, no radius, tracking `0.16em`, uppercase
- **Secondary** — outline `1px amber-700`, text `amber-900`, transparent fill
- **Ghost** — text only with underline on hover, `amber-700`
- **Text-link** — inline, `amber-700`, underline

### Input field
- Height 48px
- Border bottom only (`1px amber-300`), transparent fill
- Label above, serif italic
- Error state: border-bottom `error-500`, helper text below

### Product card
- Square image top (1:1)
- Name (serif, 20px)
- Descriptor (italic, 14px, 2 lines max)
- Size chips (pill, 11px uppercase)
- Price range
- CTA: text-link `VIEW →`

### Service card
- Icon or numeral (01, 02, …) in `amber-500` at 40px
- Name (serif, 24px)
- Descriptor (italic, 14px, 2 lines)
- `BOOK` text-link

### Review card
- Quote mark glyph (large, `amber-300`, top-left)
- Quote text (italic serif)
- Attribution (ui-sans, small-caps, `ink-500`)
- Star row (5 stars, `amber-500`)

### Section header
- Eyebrow (ui-sans, 11px, 0.2em tracking, uppercase, `ink-500`)
- H2 (display-serif, 48px desktop / 32px mobile)
- Italic word in H2 is always the last 1–2 words

### Floating CTA rail
- Vertical stack of 3 circular 44px buttons
- Background `amber-500`, icons `cream-50`
- 12px gap between
- Bottom-right on desktop (24px offset), bottom-center on mobile full-width bar

### Footer email capture
- Background `amber-100`
- 48px vertical padding
- Label `SKIN NOTES` (ui-sans eyebrow)
- Microcopy serif italic
- Inline field + button

## 5. Imagery direction

- Warm, natural light, never over-saturated
- Existing assets: two selfies of Amber, product flyer, purple/peach painted mural from FB banner
- Use existing FB graphics as reference: soft floral frames, cursive script, cherry-blossom and rose-leaf motifs
- Product shots: 1:1, amber glass bottles on cream or neutral linen, sprig of herb or crystal beside
- Avoid stock "spa with towels and cucumbers"
- Avoid white-room minimalism — warmth matters

### Image treatments
- Slight grain overlay (5–8% noise) for warmth
- Drop-shadow only on product cards, never on photos
- Photo corners: `radius-none` on hero, `radius-md` on cards

---

## 6. Motion

- Page transitions: none (instant)
- Hover: 120ms ease-out, color + underline only
- CTA press: 80ms scale to 0.98
- Scroll reveal: 300ms fade + 12px rise, triggered at 10% visible
- Floating CTA: 200ms slide-up on first scroll, never bouncing
- No parallax. No hero video autoplay. Reserve motion.

---

## 7. Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA minimum)

- Contrast: `amber-900` on `cream-50` = 11.2:1 ✓ · `amber-500` on `cream-50` = 4.8:1 ✓ · Never pair `amber-300` with `cream-50` for text
- Tap targets: 44×44px minimum everywhere
- Focus rings: 2px `amber-700` outline, 2px offset — never removed
- Form labels always visible (no placeholder-only labels)
- Alt text required on every photo (see sitemap file §URL conventions)
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: disable all scroll-reveal, keep hovers
- Keyboard: full tab order, skip-to-content link at top
