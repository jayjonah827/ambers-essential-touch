# Amber's hands as the defining product

## Strategic statement

Amber's commercial differentiator is not a generic skincare bottle. It is trained, attentive, consultation-led touch. The owner's phrase, “Amber's product is her hands,” should organize future service storytelling.

## What the source set supports

- Amber is a licensed esthetician and long-term practice owner.
- Sources describe consultation, precision, aftercare, waxing, facials, skin analysis, manicure, sanitation, documentation, and client care.
- Existing imagery includes Amber portraits, social treatment context, nails, service spaces, and hand motifs.
- The voice system emphasizes being held, intentional touch, ritual, patience, and preservation.

## Required asset study before production

1. Obtain an owner-approved, high-resolution photograph of Amber's actual hand and nails in neutral light.
2. Record which hand, nail shape, polish, jewelry, marks, and pose are identity-significant.
3. Produce separate studies: simplified vector contour, airbrush-gradient silhouette, photographic cutout, and motion path.
4. Test each study over warm light pink, sunlit botanicals, moisture gradients, and editorial treatment photography.
5. Confirm legibility, accessibility, cultural tone, and small-size behavior.
6. Obtain explicit approval for the chosen hand asset and its permitted contexts.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- No generic stock or generated hand presented as Amber's.
- No hand inferred from a face-only portrait.
- No extra fingers, altered nails, changed skin tone, or identity smoothing.
- No sexualized touch framing.
- No medical outcome, inflammation, or healing claim attached to the hand motif without substantiation.
- No compositing onto a client or model image without rights and consent.

## Future system role

Once approved, the hand asset can become a consistent service-device: framing editorial photographs, guiding scroll motion, indicating consultation steps, revealing before/after education, and distinguishing Amber's practice from packaged-product competitors. That is a future design phase, not authorization to generate the final asset now.
