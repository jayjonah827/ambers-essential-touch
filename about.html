Origin

How I got here.

Skin has been my study for nearly three decades. I started because my own skin taught me that not every product is built for every body — and most of them aren't. So I learned. I trained. I got licensed. And then I kept going, because licensing is a beginning, not a destination.

Over eighteen years, I've worked on skin of every color, age, and condition. I've learned that the fastest result is rarely the right one. That aftercare is the treatment. That a good ritual outlasts a good product.

Philosophy

This is not fast beauty.

There is no rush here. Skin responds to patience, repetition, and respect. Results come from consistency, not shortcuts. Slow care lasts longer.
